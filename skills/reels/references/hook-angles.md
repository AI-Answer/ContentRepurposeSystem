# Film-Hook Angles

Choose three distinct angles per reel. Each line must fit within 15 words and 4 seconds when spoken. These are examples, not claims to paste into every plan. Use a line only when the reel delivers its promise.

## Contrarian

Challenge a common habit. Show the proof right after the line.

- "Stop giving Claude longer prompts. Give it one clear job."
- "You don't need another AI tool. Fix your workflow first."

## Result / Proof

Lead with something the viewer can see. Use a real result from the source video.

- "Claude turned this video into three posts. Here's the output."
- "I gave Claude my notes. It built this working page."

## Curiosity Gap

Open a question the reel answers. Make the topic clear in the first line.

- "Claude kept missing this step. One file fixed it."
- "This Claude setting changed how I start every project."

## Call Out the Viewer

Name the person and the problem. Speak to one viewer.

- "If you repeat the same Claude prompt, save it as a skill."
- "Still copying AI answers by hand? Let Claude save the file."

## Score Before Filming

Use the hooks skill's core desire, proxy desire, power words, and Four Commandments. Keep the scores in `repurpose-plan.md`. Put the selected desire and angle in `framework`, the visual and delivery in `shot_note`, and the short overlay in `on_screen_text`. Spoken words, visuals, and text must promise the same payoff.
